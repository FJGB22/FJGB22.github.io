Mika — AI VTuber

A real-time, voice-driven AI streamer with her own personality, memory, and sense of timing.

View repo ↗

What it does

Mika runs as a simulated livestream: viewer messages come in as chat, and she answers out loud — sarcastic and teasing, not a polite assistant. You can talk to her by voice and she talks back. If chat goes quiet, she starts talking on her own, like a real streamer filling dead air.

How it's built

  • Streaming brain — LLM tokens are streamed and cut into sentences, so speech can start before the full reply is finished.
  • Interruptible voice — an audio queue that can be cut off mid-sentence when someone speaks.
  • Ears — microphone input transcribed locally with Whisper.
  • Director — handles turn-taking and idle behaviour.
  • Memory — recalls facts across turns within a session.

What I learned

I built a measurement bench before optimising anything. It showed Mika takes about 4.4 s before she makes a sound, and that my sentence-streaming trick saved nothing yet: the current Gemini endpoint returns each reply in one piece. The next fix isn't a bigger model. It's the ~3 s before the first sentence exists and the 1–2 s the voice takes to start.
